## What to expect at your first event

- **Bring a laptop and something to work on.** A side project, a course, interview prep. Anything counts.
- **You don't need to know anyone.** Say hi to one person and ask what they're building.
- **Pick a format you like.** Weekly coding sessions, LeetCode Wednesdays, AI paper discussions, workshops, and a hackathon every quarter.
- **Share what you're building in Discord afterwards.** This is how you find collaborators.

## Once you're settled

- **Keep coming back.** Pick one recurring session and commit to it. Consistency is how you build real relationships and make visible progress.
- **Read the Master Resources doc.** It's pinned in Discord: learning paths, tools, project ideas, and community links.
- **Bring a friend.** The community grows one connection at a time.
- **Help run things.** Want to lead a workshop or help organize? Reach out in Discord.
- **Follow along.** We post on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/c-o-d-ee) and [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/coffeecodephilly/).
