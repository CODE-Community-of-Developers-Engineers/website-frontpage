The Next Web published a feature on Tony Siu, founder of C.O.D.E, and his approach to building AI-enabled developer communities through human connection and servitude leadership.

<!--more-->

The article looks at how C.O.D.E grew from informal coding sessions into a collaborative Philadelphia network where developers, designers, founders, and builders gather to learn, experiment, and exchange ideas.

It also highlights how AI supports the community's operations, from event coordination to sponsor management and reporting, while the core value still comes from in-person trust, shared learning, and practical collaboration.
