This policy explains how C.O.D.E. Philadelphia handles information when you use [codephilly.com](https://www.codephilly.com/).

## C.O.D.E. Google Ads

C.O.D.E. Google Ads is an internal tool that C.O.D.E. uses to manage its own Google Ad Grants advertising account. It is not available to the public. The tool uses a service account to read campaign settings and performance data and to update campaigns. It writes operation outputs, error records, and optimizer logs to C.O.D.E.'s operator systems. C.O.D.E. shares Google Ads account data with OpenAI when its Codex CMO analyzes account performance. The tool does not provide visitors with Google account access.

## Information collected through this site

We use the Google tag to measure advertising conversions. The tag may send Google information about your visit, including pages viewed, your browser and device, online identifiers such as cookies, and your approximate location inferred from your IP address. When you use a link that takes you to an event, registration, or application page, the site can record that link as a conversion.

We do not offer user accounts. Third-party services host donation and registration forms. We may receive information that those services provide when you register or donate. If you choose to contact us by email, we receive the information you include in your message.

## How we use information

We use conversion measurement to understand whether people reach our community through our advertising. We use email messages to answer the request that you send us.

## Services outside this site

This site links to or embeds services run by other organizations. They have their own privacy practices and may collect information when you use them. These services include [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/legal/privacy/), [Discord](https://discord.com/privacy), [Luma](https://lu.ma/privacy), [Devpost](https://devpost.com/legal/privacy), [Google Forms](https://policies.google.com/privacy), and [Givebutter](https://givebutter.com/privacy).

## Your choices

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. You can also use Google's [Ad Settings](https://myadcenter.google.com/) to manage personalized advertising.
