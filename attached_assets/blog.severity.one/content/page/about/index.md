# About Me

## Hey there, fellow security enthusiast!

I’m **William Haesemeyer**, a **Senior Security Engineer at Jamf**, where I focus on **security automation** and making security actually work for people—not just checkboxes.

I started in IT, fixing things and pushing deadlines, before transitioning to security, where I now help protect the very systems I used to troubleshoot. That journey gave me a unique perspective:  
🔹 I understand the **pressure of getting things done fast**  
🔹 I also know **why security sometimes has to slow things down**  

My goal? **Find the right balance so both sides win.**

## Why "Severity.One"?

In incident management, a **SEV1** or **Severity 1** incident signifies a **critical, high-impact issue** requiring immediate attention. These are the system outages or major failures that disrupt everything.

At one point in my career, I was responsible for **system monitoring and managing outages**, meaning I had to **escalate, coordinate, and resolve SEV1 incidents** in real time. It was a trial by fire, but it taught me more than any textbook ever could.

I started **Severity.One** to share those lessons—so others can learn from real-world experiences **without having to go through their own SEV1 nightmare.**
