C.O.D.E (Community of Developers & Engineers) is a group of programmers, designers, creatives, and everyone in between, coming together to build together. Founded in 2024, we are a Philadelphia nonprofit run by a passionate group of volunteers dedicated to building an inclusive tech community. We organize meetups, hackathons, and networking events to help developers of all skill levels connect, learn, and grow together.
