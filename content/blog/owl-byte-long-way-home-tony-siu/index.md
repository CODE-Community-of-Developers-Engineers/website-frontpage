Temple University's Owl Byte podcast published an interview with Tony Siu on his journey from exchange student to AI engineer and founder of C.O.D.E.

<!--more-->

The episode covers Tony's path through Temple, computer vision research, multimodal AI, startup work, and the community-building story behind Code & Coffee Philadelphia.

It also shares advice for computer science students: focus on real problems, take ownership of your path, use university resources fully, and build authentic networks by sharing what excites you.
