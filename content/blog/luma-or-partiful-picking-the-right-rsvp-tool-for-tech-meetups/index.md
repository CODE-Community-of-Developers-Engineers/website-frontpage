If you run a tech meetup, a hackathon or a weekly coding night, your event platform shapes who shows up. Luma and Partiful are two of the most common tools for sending an invite link and collecting RSVPs. They look similar at first. They split on fees, discovery and how well each one supports a recurring community.

<!--more-->

I organize events for a developer community in Philadelphia, so I care less about party themes and more about repeat attendance, check-in and what happens when an event charges money. This comparison stays on the questions an organizer, sponsor or attendee will actually face when choosing between the two.

In plain terms, Luma is an event management platform built around calendars, registration and ticketing. Partiful is a social invitation app built around shareable invite pages, guest lists and a lively RSVP experience. Both let guests RSVP from one link, and both work for free events.

> Quick verdict: Luma suits recurring, professional or ticketed tech events, such as meetups, workshops and hackathons. Partiful suits casual, social gatherings where the invite itself sets the mood and no ticket revenue is involved.

## At a glance: how Luma and Partiful compare

Luma is the stronger fit for structured, recurring and paid events, while Partiful is the stronger fit for social invites with a playful feel. The table below sums up the axes this comparison turns on.

Luma vs Partiful on the points that matter for tech events

| Platform | Paid ticket fees | RSVP experience | Discovery and calendars | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Luma | [5%](https://help.luma.com/p/luma-vs-meetup) platform fee plus Stripe on free plan; 0% platform fee on Luma Plus | Clean registration forms, approvals, check-in | Public calendars that people subscribe to | Meetups, workshops, hackathons |
| Partiful | [10%](https://help.luma.com/p/luma-vs-partiful) + $2.00 per ticket on reviewed public events | Themed invite pages, text-message style updates | Mostly invite-link driven with some discovery | Parties, social and casual gatherings |

## Ticket fees and paid events

Luma is cheaper for ticketed events in nearly every scenario the published numbers cover. The gap grows quickly once a hackathon or workshop charges even a modest fee.

**Luma** On the free plan, [paid tickets incur a 5% platform fee plus Stripe processing (2.9% + 30¢)](https://help.luma.com/p/luma-vs-meetup). Organizers who run many paid events can upgrade: [with Luma Plus ($59/mo annually or $69/mo monthly), the platform fee drops to 0%](https://help.luma.com/p/luma-vs-meetup), leaving only payment processing. Luma's own comparison puts it this way: [on 100 tickets at $20, that's $88 in fees on Luma Plus versus about $400 on Partiful](https://help.luma.com/p/luma-vs-partiful). That figure comes from Luma itself, so treat it as a vendor's math and check it against your own ticket mix.

**Partiful** Partiful does support paid tickets. According to Luma's review, "Across the public ticketed events we reviewed on Partiful, the standard fee was 10% + $2.00 per ticket" ([Luma Help](https://help.luma.com/p/luma-vs-partiful)). The fixed [$2.00](https://help.luma.com/p/luma-vs-partiful) matters most on cheap tickets, where it can exceed the percentage portion. For a free event, none of this applies, and Partiful's fee structure is irrelevant.

For a nonprofit that charges a small fee to reduce no-shows, the fee model is the deciding factor. Every dollar that goes to platform fees is a dollar that does not go to food, venue costs or prizes for participants.

## RSVP flow and the guest experience

Partiful makes RSVPing feel social and fun. Luma makes RSVPing feel orderly and dependable. Which one is better depends on whether your guests are friends or strangers.

**Luma** Luma's registration lets an organizer add custom questions, such as experience level or team status, and require approval before a guest is confirmed. That helps for hackathons with limited seats or venue rules. Guests get calendar invites and reminders, and the organizer gets a check-in list at the door. The pages look polished but plain, which reads well for a workshop sponsored by a company.

**Partiful** Partiful's pitch is one link, and everyone's in. Hosts create an invite with themes and effects, guests RSVP with a phone number, and everyone stays on the same page through text updates and a shared activity feed. Guests can see who else is going, which pushes attendance at social events. Its reach is real: [in Q1 2025, Partiful reported 500K monthly active users, up 400% YoY](https://hiddenevents.online/blog/luma-vs-meetup-vs-eventbrite-vs-partiful/). By 2026, many people in their twenties already have the app, so a Partiful link feels familiar to them.

## Discovery and recurring community tools

Luma is built for organizers who host the same type of event again and again. Partiful is built mainly around individual invites, though it has added ways to find events.

**Luma** Luma lets a group run a public calendar. People subscribe once and hear about every new session, which fits a weekly coding night well. The scale is large: [about 2 million people sign up for events on the platform every month](https://party.pro/luma/), according to Party.pro. Many tech, AI and startup events in large cities list on Luma, so builders already browse it to find things to attend.

**Partiful** Partiful's home page tagline is to create invites and discover events, and the app does surface events from friends and public hosts. Most attendance still comes from the host sharing a link in group chats. It has public recognition behind it: [in 2024, Google named it one of their Best Apps of the year, and in 2025, TIME named Partiful one of its TIME100 Most Influential Companies](https://party.pro/partiful/). It is well funded, having [raised $27.3 million in total funding](https://party.pro/partiful/), with a [~$140M valuation](https://hiddenevents.online/blog/luma-vs-meetup-vs-eventbrite-vs-partiful/) reported by Hidden Events.

Neither tool runs a hackathon's submissions or judging. For that, organizers usually pair the RSVP tool with a project platform such as [Devpost](https://devpost.com), or join a league such as MLH, which supports student hackathons. Groups like [Cerebral Valley](https://cerebralvalley.ai) list AI events, and Techadelphia covers the Philadelphia tech scene.

## What sponsors and attendees should notice

Sponsors and donors care about the platform because it affects reporting and cost. Attendees care because it affects how easy it is to show up.

**Luma** Luma's registration data, including custom answers and check-in records, makes it simpler for an organizer to report attendance to a sponsor or CSR program manager. If you sponsor an event listed on Luma, ask the organizer for check-in counts rather than RSVP counts, since those show who actually came.

**Partiful** Partiful's strength is turnout energy. For an after-party, a sponsor mixer or a casual social, the guest list and comments make people more likely to attend. It gives organizers less structured data, so a sponsor should agree on how attendance will be reported before the event.

## Verdict

Luma wins for structured tech events, and Partiful wins for social ones. Many communities use both: Luma for the main program and Partiful for informal gatherings.

- Choose Luma if you run recurring meetups and want people to subscribe to a calendar.
- Choose Luma if your event charges for tickets and you want lower fees.
- Choose Luma if you need approval steps, custom questions or check-in data for sponsors.
- Choose Partiful if the event is a casual social and your guests already use the app.
- Choose Partiful if the invite's look and the visible guest list will drive turnout.
- Choose Partiful if the event is free and small, so fees never come up.

> Verdict: Luma is the better pick for meetups, workshops, hackathons and any paid tech event. Partiful is the better pick for free, casual, friend-driven gatherings.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I sell tickets on Partiful?

Yes. Partiful supports paid tickets. On public ticketed events Luma reviewed, [the standard fee was 10% + $2.00 per ticket](https://help.luma.com/p/luma-vs-partiful).

### Can you find events on Partiful?

Yes, Partiful has a discovery side that shows events from friends and public hosts. Most guests still arrive through a link the host shares.

### Is Partiful free to use?

Partiful is free for creating invites and collecting RSVPs on free events. Fees apply only when you sell tickets.
